## Prospective-investor update

**Subject:** {Company}, {month}: {headline}

Three lines: the headline, the two metrics that best show trajectory, and what the next 90 days will prove. Same day each month. No ask until the raise begins.

## Diligence answer

1. The direct answer in the first sentence.
2. The evidence: numbers, cohorts, examples.
3. The limits: what is not yet proven and how you are testing it.
4. One sentence on why it matters to the business.

## Advisor note

The single ask in the first sentence, the context in two sentences, the forwardable blurb if an introduction is involved, and thanks for the last thing they did.
