# Rewrite bank

Before-and-after pairs for each retired pattern. Use them as models, not fixed wording: the best rewrite for a line depends on the sentences around it. Add new pairs whenever a good rewrite turns up.

## 1. Fragment with a tag

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Four layers, built in order. | The authority stack has four layers, and each one rests on the layer beneath it. |
| A rhythm your team can hold: long pieces on your own site, the same argument on social in a founder's voice, and nurture that keeps you present between conversations. | Publishing compounds when your team can sustain it, which in practice means a long piece on your own site, the same argument on social in a founder's voice, and nurture email that keeps you present between conversations. |
| The claim only you can make, drawn from your closed deals rather than from what already ranks. | Position is the claim only you can make, and it comes from your closed deals instead of from what already ranks. |
| The evidence that makes the position believable: named sources, customer situations, worked examples and numbers somebody else can check. | The position becomes believable through evidence such as named sources, customer situations, worked examples and numbers somebody else can check. |
| Customer stories told as a problem somebody had and what changed, rather than a testimonial nobody believes | Customer stories describe a problem somebody had and what changed, instead of offering a testimonial nobody believes |
| Working with a B2B content agency builds authority in the field you sell into: a clear point of view, published consistently, backed by evidence, and written to be quoted. | Working with a B2B content agency builds authority in the field you sell into, through a clear point of view that is published consistently, backed by evidence and written to be quoted. |

## Whole-paragraph example

**Before** (written in the style being retired):

> Three layers, built in order. Positioning first, then proof, then publishing, so each piece has a job. None of it works without a clear point of view.

**After:**

> The work has three layers, starting with positioning because every later piece repeats it. Proof comes next, in the form of named sources and customer situations a buyer can check, and publishing follows once the argument and its evidence are settled. Without a clear point of view the later layers have nothing to carry, which is why the position is agreed before anything is written.

## A note on repairs that create new tells

The "after" column should not repeat one construction. If three consecutive rewrites all end in ", which means", change two of them to an effect verb or a condition-first sentence.
