## The event in one paragraph

Pavilion GTM Summit, Sep 28 – Oct 1, 2026, The Glasshouse, New York. "Come for the strategies. Stay for the conversations." ~1,000 VP+ GTM operators in B2B tech and SaaS; every attendee is vetted for VP-level or above. Pavilion frames it as "the room for people in the arena" — the one thing AI can't do is lead. Four days: Women's Summit (Mon), Circles (Tue), two GTM main days (Wed–Thu). All-day programming each day plus after-hours events.

## Venue — The Glasshouse

- Address: 660 12th Avenue, Floor 6, New York, NY 10019. West Side Highway between W 48th and W 49th St, Hudson Yards / Hell's Kitchen waterfront. Phone +1 212 242 7800, info@theglasshouses.com.
- Scale: 75,000 sq ft total (50,000 event level + 25,000 ancillary). Two main halls: The Suite (14,000 sq ft, 1,850 theater / 1,380 cocktail) and The Studio (10,900 sq ft, 1,500 theater / 1,050 cocktail), joined by an acoustic retracting partition. 30 ft ceilings, 49 programmable chandeliers, floor-to-ceiling glass, 360° city and Hudson River views.
- Outdoors: two open-air terraces flow directly from the main event space — the natural place for breaks and calls.
- Rooms used by GTM2026 (from the session export): Main Stage, Spotlight Stage, Breakout 1, Breakout 2, Exhibitor Hall (Wed–Thu). Tuesday Circles run in parallel across Main Stage, Breakout 1, Breakout 2 and the 5th Floor Breakout Room. Spotlight Stage sits in or beside the Exhibitor Hall; Spotlight talks run during breaks.
- VIP lounge exists on-site (full bar, private space); GTM2026 VIP tickets add lounge access, a private snack bar and exclusive events.
- Green rooms with shower suites — irrelevant to attendees unless speaking.
- Accessibility: elevators to Floor 6 (venue is a modern build); no published accessibility statement from the organizer `verify`.

## Getting there

- Ride-share drop-off (official): 12th Ave & W 48th St.
- Subway: closest is 50 St (C/E) at 8th Ave, ~12–15 min walk west; 42 St–Port Authority (A/C/E/N/Q/R/W/1/2/3/7) ~15–20 min `verify`. Nothing runs to 12th Ave itself.
- Bus: M50 and M12 stop at W 49th St & 12th Ave, 2 min walk.
- Airports (official estimates): LGA ~25 min (closest), EWR ~45 min, JFK ~45–60 min (best for international).
- Parking: commercial garages along W 43rd–W 50th between 10th and 12th Ave `verify`; expect Manhattan rates.
- Weather: late September NYC, typically 60–72°F / 16–22°C, terrace-friendly; carry a layer.

## Hotels and travel

- Official guidance: book through Navan Edge (flights, hotels, trains in one place; $100 Amazon gift card incentive after the trip). No named room block on the public site `verify`.
- Practical radius: Hell's Kitchen / Hudson Yards / Midtown West hotels are a 10–20 min walk; anything near Times Square or Penn Station is a short ride.

## Tickets

- Paid event; Standard and VIP. VIP adds lounge access, private snack bar, exclusive events. Prices not published on the public site — the register page routes to the form.
- Included with every ticket: keynotes, breakouts, roundtables, community events, breakfast, lunch, snacks, daily networking happy hours.
- Circles and Women's Summit are separate purchases, first come first served.
- Non-refundable after Aug 23, 2026. Transfers to qualified executives allowed up to 7 days before.
- Dress: business casual.
