# Methodology & Diagnostic Thresholds

Evaluate enterprise LinkedIn campaign performance using four quantitative tests. A campaign is classified as **Healthy & Scalable** only if it satisfies all four conditions.

## Test 1: Account Coverage & Reach Depth
* **Healthy Threshold:** At least 60% of total target accounts in the designated ABM list receive at least one impression during the campaign evaluation window.
* **Critical Threshold:** Less than 30% of target accounts are reached while average frequency per persona climbs, indicating budget concentration on a narrow subset.

## Test 2: Audience Saturation & Frequency
* **Healthy Threshold:** Average exposure frequency remains balanced alongside stable click-through rates.
* **Critical Threshold:** Average exposure frequency per persona exceeds 10 impressions within a 30-day window, accompanied by a drop in engagement rates of more than 25% and rising cost per target account reached.

## Test 3: Form Factor Quality Ratio
* **Healthy Threshold:** Lead-to-opportunity conversion rate for native Lead Gen Forms reaches at least 40% of the conversion rate observed for website demo requests on matching audience segments.
* **Critical Threshold:** Native Lead Gen Form conversion to qualified opportunity drops below 20% of website demo request performance, indicating lead generation friction removal at the expense of buyer intent.

## Test 4: CAC vs. Contract Value Sustainability
* **Healthy Threshold:** Media Cost per Acquired Customer (calculated as Cost per Opportunity divided by Opportunity-to-Win rate, plus creative production overhead) remains below 20% of Annual Contract Value (ACV) multiplied by gross margin, with payback achieved within 12 months.
* **Critical Threshold:** Media Cost per Acquired Customer exceeds 35% of gross-margin-adjusted ACV, or payback period exceeds 18 months.

## Verdict Criteria

* **Healthy & Scalable:** Test 1 Passed + Test 2 Passed + Test 3 Passed + Test 4 Passed. Action: Maintain target criteria, test new creative variations, and scale budget incrementally.
* **Audience/Quality Failure:** Test 1 Failed OR Test 2 Failed OR Test 3 Failed OR Test 4 Failed. Action: Disable audience expansion, review form qualification criteria, and adjust frequency caps.
* **Inconclusive Data:** Account-level attribution untracked or evaluation window shorter than median sales cycle. Action: Suspend verdict and flag data collection requirements.
