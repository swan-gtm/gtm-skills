# Red Flags & Disqualification Criteria

Identify immediate disqualification factors that invalidate standard organic traffic reporting and indicate underlying strategy failures.

## Critical Red Flags

### 1. Intent Page Isolation Failure
* **Indicator:** Less than 3% of total organic sessions touch commercial intent pages (e.g., pricing, demo request, customer stories, product features) during their session journey.
* **Diagnosis:** The site attracts informational readers without capturing buying intent. Session growth is decoupled from revenue pipeline.

### 2. Low-Intent Lead Contamination
* **Indicator:** Non-business email domain form submissions (e.g., personal email domains, student accounts, job-seeker patterns) exceed 25% of total organic form fills.
* **Diagnosis:** Content architecture targets overly generic queries, attracting non-buyer personas and inflating lead metrics without generating sales-qualified pipeline.

### 3. Non-Human Traffic Contamination
* **Indicator:** Sudden traffic spikes characterized by near-zero session duration, geographic misalignment with target sales regions, or isolated single-page hits without subsequent interaction events.
* **Diagnosis:** Automated scraping, AI agent crawling, or bot traffic is inflating organic baselines. All historical baseline comparisons are invalid until data filtering is applied.

## Blinding Condition: Missing Pipeline Attribution

If the organization cannot track or report the number of qualified opportunities generated by organic touchpoints:
* **Status:** Blinding Condition Active.
* **Directive:** Do not issue traffic optimization recommendations, technical audits, or content strategy changes.
* **Mandatory Next Step:** Issue an explicit request to configure multi-touch or primary source attribution before proceeding with organic analysis.
