# Methodology & Diagnostic Thresholds

Evaluate organic performance changes using four quantitative tests. A traffic decline is categorized as **Healthy Zero-Click Shift** only if it satisfies all four conditions.

## Test 1: Impression Stability
* **Healthy Threshold:** Impression volume on identical query clusters stays within a +/- 5% variance, or increases, while click volume decreases. This indicates sustained visibility with direct-on-SERP answer consumption.
* **Critical Threshold:** Impression volume declines by more than 15% across matching query clusters. This indicates true loss of search engine visibility.

## Test 2: Intent Composition Variance
* **Healthy Threshold:** More than 80% of total click loss is concentrated in Non-Brand Informational query buckets (e.g., definitions, top-of-funnel concepts), while Non-Brand Commercial queries (e.g., pricing, alternatives, software comparisons) decline by less than 5%.
* **Critical Threshold:** Non-Brand Commercial query clicks decline by more than 10%, indicating pipeline risk.

## Test 3: Brand Equity Balance
* **Healthy Threshold:** Brand search clicks constitute between 30% and 50% of total organic traffic.
* **Critical Threshold (Over-reliance):** Brand search share exceeds 70% of total organic traffic alongside declining non-brand impressions, indicating failure in new demand creation.
* **Critical Threshold (Weak Brand):** Brand search share falls below 15% of total organic traffic, amplifying the impact of informational search shifts.

## Test 4: Pipeline Correlation
* **Healthy Threshold:** Organic session volume decreases by more than 20% while organic-attributed demo requests and opportunity creation remain within a +/- 3% variance.
* **Critical Threshold:** Organic-attributed pipeline declines at a rate equal to or greater than the percentage drop in organic session volume.

## Verdict Criteria

* **Healthy Zero-Click Shift:** Test 1 Passed + Test 2 Passed + Test 3 Passed + Test 4 Passed. Action: Focus on zero-click content authority, brand mentions in AI outputs, and intent page conversion optimization.
* **Critical Visibility Failure:** Test 1 Failed OR Test 2 Failed OR Test 4 Failed. Action: Execute technical recovery, re-evaluate intent alignment, and review core search indexation.
* **Inconclusive Data:** Pipeline tracking unverified or evaluation window shorter than three months. Action: Suspend verdict and issue data collection request.
