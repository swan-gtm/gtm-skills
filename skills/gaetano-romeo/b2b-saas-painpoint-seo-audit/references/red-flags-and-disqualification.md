# Red Flags & Disqualification Criteria

Identify immediate disqualification factors that invalidate standard content marketing reporting and indicate misaligned editorial priorities.

## Critical Red Flags

### 1. Volume-Driven Editorial Calendar Alignment
* **Indicator:** Editorial topics are selected exclusively based on keyword monthly search volume metrics, with no mapping to customer pain points, sales objections, or competitive alternative searches.
* **Diagnosis:** The content strategy optimizes for traffic volume rather than buyer acquisition, creating an audience profile mismatched with the ICP.

### 2. High-Traffic Pipeline Isolation
* **Indicator:** The top 5 highest-traffic organic URLs on the website generate 0 qualified CRM opportunities over a 6-month evaluation window.
* **Diagnosis:** Organic resources are consumed by generic informational topics that fail to capture or route commercial buying intent.

### 3. Untracked Pre-Conversion Touchpoints
* **Indicator:** The organization tracks form conversions globally without recording the landing page, entry URL, or path touchpoints that led to the submission.
* **Diagnosis:** Content evaluation is blind to revenue attribution. Session metrics and goal completions cannot be connected to specific editorial investments.

## Blinding Condition: Missing URL Touchpoint Tracking

If organic form conversions cannot be mapped to preceding URL touchpoint paths:
* **Status:** Blinding Condition Active.
* **Directive:** Do not issue editorial calendar changes, content pruning recommendations, or keyword reallocation directives.
* **Mandatory Next Step:** Implement pre-conversion URL path tracking in analytics and CRM systems before evaluating content architecture performance.
