# Edge Cases & Attribution Anomalies

When analyzing B2B SaaS pain-point content performance, resolve two primary structural edge cases before finalizing a diagnostic verdict.

## Edge Case 1: Low Search Volume High-Intent Pain Points

High-intent pain-point queries (e.g., specific software migration errors, niche integration workarounds) often register zero or negligible search volume in standard SEO tools.

### Analysis Protocol:
1. Cross-reference internal sales call transcripts, customer support tickets, and lost-deal reason codes to identify real buyer friction points.
2. Publish dedicated solution assets regardless of external search volume estimates.
3. Evaluate page success strictly on opportunity influence and deal velocity rather than session volume.
4. Do not prune or deprecate low-traffic pages if they appear in pre-conversion URL touchpoint logs for enterprise opportunities.

## Edge Case 2: ToFu Informational Assets as Secondary Nurturing Touchpoints

Informational blog posts may show low direct conversion rates while serving as critical touchpoints during retargeting or organic multi-visit buyer journeys.

### Analysis Protocol:
1. Analyze multi-touch attribution paths to determine whether informational assets appear as secondary touchpoints prior to direct demo requests.
2. Calculate the proportion of returning organic visitors who first touched an informational asset before later converting on a commercial page.
3. Preserve high-traffic informational assets that demonstrate clear secondary touchpoint value, focusing optimization on internal contextual linking to relevant BoFu pages.
