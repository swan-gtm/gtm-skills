Audit B2B SaaS organic content architecture by shifting focus from keyword search volume to buyer intent, isolating bottom-of-funnel (BoFu) pain points, evaluating comparison page integrity, and measuring pipeline contribution across funnel stages.

## Quick verdict

Execute a two-stage evaluation before revising editorial calendars or keyword priorities.

### Stage 1: Funnel intent distribution and pipeline density
* Categorize all traffic-generating organic pages into three stages: Buyer Pain-Point Solution Pages, Informational Learning Assets, and Unaligned Traffic Collectors.
* Map CRM opportunity touchpoints against each page category.
* If top traffic pages generate zero opportunity touchpoints while a small cluster of BoFu pages drives all pipeline, label as **Vanity Traffic Bias**.
* If BoFu search footprint is absent, label as **Intent Void**.

### Stage 2: Comparison integrity and conversion asymmetry
* Evaluate conversion rate ratios between BoFu pages (e.g., `Brand vs Competitor`, alternatives, pricing) and top-of-funnel (ToFu) informational articles.
* Audit comparison pages for objective differentiation vs. promotional biased feature checklists.

## The four tests

A content strategy is classified as **Pipeline-Optimized** only if it satisfies all four conditions. Full thresholds are in `references/methodology-and-thresholds.md`.

| Test | Healthy | Critical |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pipeline density ratio | At least 50% of organic-attributed pipeline comes from High-Intent Pain-Point or Comparison pages, even if they are less than 15% of site traffic | Less than 10% of organic pipeline is generated by high-intent commercial pages |
| 2. Conversion rate asymmetry | High-intent pages convert to qualified demo requests or trial sign-ups at a significantly higher rate than informational posts (e.g., 5x to 10x) | Conversion rates across high-intent pages and informational posts remain flat |
| 3. Comparison page neutrality and trust | Comparison pages state specific use cases where competing products excel, keep verifiable feature criteria, and give clear trade-off analyses | Comparison pages present one-sided feature checklists with uniform positive marks for the proprietary product |
| 4. Business domain qualification rate | Form submissions from high-intent pain-point pages have a business email domain rate of at least 80% | Non-business email submissions exceed 35% on commercial intent assets |

## Verdict

* **Pipeline-Optimized:** Test 1 Passed + Test 2 Passed + Test 3 Passed + Test 4 Passed. Action: Scale high-intent content production, expand competitor comparison footprints, and optimize mid-funnel conversion paths.
* **Volume-Biased Failure:** Test 1 Failed OR Test 2 Failed OR Test 4 Failed. Action: Reallocate editorial budget from generic informational terms to specific pain-point solution pages.
* **Inconclusive Data:** CRM URL touchpoint history untracked or evaluation window shorter than three months. Action: Suspend verdict and issue tracking requirement.

## Before finalizing

* Resolve the two structural edge cases in `references/edge-cases-and-attribution.md`: low search volume high-intent pain points, and ToFu informational assets as secondary nurturing touchpoints.
* Check the red flags and the blinding condition in `references/red-flags-and-disqualification.md`.

## What good looks like

* **Touchpoint tracking comes first.** If organic form conversions cannot be mapped to preceding URL touchpoint paths, do not issue editorial calendar changes, content pruning recommendations, or keyword reallocation directives. Implement pre-conversion URL path tracking in analytics and CRM systems first.
* **Pain points come from the business, not the keyword tool.** High-intent queries often register zero or negligible search volume, so sales call transcripts, customer support tickets, and lost-deal reason codes are cross-referenced to find real buyer friction, and pages are judged on opportunity influence and deal velocity rather than session volume.
* **Nothing is pruned on traffic alone.** Low-traffic pages that appear in pre-conversion touchpoint logs for enterprise opportunities stay, and high-traffic informational assets with clear secondary touchpoint value are preserved and linked to relevant BoFu pages.
* **The common mistake:** selecting editorial topics exclusively on monthly search volume, with no mapping to customer pain points, sales objections, or competitive alternative searches. The strategy optimizes for traffic volume rather than buyer acquisition.
* **The tell that it has gone wrong:** the top 5 highest-traffic organic URLs generate 0 qualified CRM opportunities over a 6-month window.
