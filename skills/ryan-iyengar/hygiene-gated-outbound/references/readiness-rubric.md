# Launch readiness rubric

Use these starting thresholds for the exact campaign slice:

- Duplicate account risk: under 1%; no unresolved duplicate among the first launch batch.
- Ownership: 100% of targets have one active owner or deterministic route.
- Suppression checks: 100% evaluated; zero unresolved customer, active-deal, opt-out, or legal conflicts.
- Stable account relationship: at least 98% of contacts; exclude the remainder.
- Verified channel detail: at least 95% for email or 98% for a smaller high-value segment.
- Fit traceability: 100% have a recorded inclusion reason.

Return **go** when all hard gates and thresholds pass. Return **conditional go** when a clean subset passes and excluded records are isolated. Return **stop** when identity, ownership, or suppression cannot be reliably evaluated.
