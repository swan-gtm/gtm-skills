# Voice & Structure — the calibrated formula

Every rule below is derived from a 100-post performance dataset (top-15
posts vs bottom-50 by engagement). Markers that separate winners from
flops, with prevalence in top posts vs the rest:

| Marker | Top 15 | Bottom 50 |
|---|---|---|
| Comment-word CTA | 100% | 46% |
| Ellipsis line-pacing | 100% | 52% |
| P.S. line | 60% | 24% |
| "(steal X, completely free)" parenthetical | present | never |
| Specific number/tool in hook | 60% | 46% |
| Median length | ~1,300 chars | ~850 chars |

Treat the 100% markers as mandatory. Treat the rest as strong defaults.

## The five-part structure (fixed order, every post)

1. **Outcome-first hook** — a concrete result, number, or named tool in
   line one. Never a topic, never a question, never "thoughts on X".
2. **Free-claim parenthetical** — immediately under the hook, in brackets:
   "(steal the exact blueprint, completely free)". Vary the verb
   (steal/grab/swipe) and the noun; keep the shape.
3. **Context/pain block** — why this matters now. Name the ICP's specific
   pain in their own words. Often opens with "For context..." and builds
   through 4–8 short lines to a turn ("The fix isn't X. It's Y...").
4. **Deliverable stack** — the "inside:" list. Formatting rules live in
   `titles-and-deliverables.md`; follow them exactly.
5. **CTA + P.S.** — two valid formats. Single-line: "Comment "WORD" and
   I'll DM you the [thing]." Or two-step: 👉 Comment "WORD" / 👉 Connect
   with me so the DM goes through — always the low-friction comment step
   FIRST; readers do the first action they see. The P.S. is the
   second-most-read line in the post after the hook — never waste it. Best
   uses in order: a proof/asset pointer ("full sample report in the first
   comment"), a trust line ("unedited output, one chat"), or the connection
   note if the CTA didn't already carry it.

Media direction accompanies every post: the visual must *prove* the hook's
claim (dashboard screenshot, results chart, the actual resource open on
screen). A post whose image proves nothing is running four parts of a
five-part formula. For video: cold-open on the *before* state (the raw
file, the messy dashboard) — the payoff only lands after the pain is seen —
and burn the hook line in as overlay text, because most feeds autoplay
silent.

## Voice rules

- **One idea per line.** Blank line between every line or two. The post
  should read as a fast vertical scan, never paragraphs.
- **Ellipsis pacing.** Use "..." to end a line that pulls the reader to the
  next one. 2–4 per post — pacing device, not tic.
- **CAPS for emphasis** on 1–4 load-bearing words per post (ONE, EXACT,
  ENTIRE, FIRST, RARELY). Never full caps sentences except a
  pattern-interrupt hook.
- **Specific numbers everywhere.** "5 agents", "27 calls in 48 hours",
  "4.5 focused hours" — round numbers read as invented; precise ones read
  as true.
- **Length target: 1,100–1,600 characters**, roughly 20–30 rendered lines.
  Under ~900 chars reads thin; over ~1,800 loses the scan.
- **Bullets**: "-->" arrows for mechanism/how-it-works lists, "✅" checks
  for the deliverable stack, "-" dashes for quick result lists.
- **Second person pivot.** The pain block must move from "I/we" (proof) to
  "you" (their situation) before the deliverable stack.
- **No hashtags. No links in the post body.** The comment word is the whole
  conversion mechanic.
- **Comment word**: single word, CAPS, in quotes, thematically tied to the
  magnet (a systems magnet → "STACK", "AGENTS", "SOP"; a template pack →
  "TEMPLATES", "KIT").

## Pre-delivery checklist

Score every draft. Fix failures before presenting.

1. Hook contains a specific number, named tool, or hard contrast — not a
   topic
2. Free-claim parenthetical present within the first three lines
3. Pain block names the ICP's problem in their language and pivots to "you"
4. Deliverable stack passes all rules in `titles-and-deliverables.md`
5. Single comment word, quoted, CAPS; in a two-step CTA the comment step
   comes before the connect step
6. P.S. line present and working (proof pointer, trust line, or connection
   note — not filler)
7. 1,100–1,600 characters
8. At least two ellipsis pace-lines
9. Zero fabricated metrics
10. Media direction line written and it *proves the hook*
