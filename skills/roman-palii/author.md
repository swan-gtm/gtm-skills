Founder of KyivGallery, Ukraine's first online art marketplace — launched January 2007 and running ever since: 18,500+ works from 500+ artists listed over nineteen years, 5,000+ live today, shipping worldwide and trading through the war. Non-executive since 2024.

Nineteen years of marketplace economics rather than SaaS playbooks: supplier revenue splits, discount policy, and the contract terms that decide whether a promotion makes money or quietly destroys it. Alongside it, founded and exited Kuponi.com.ua, Ukraine's first coupon aggregator, and scaled a content monetization network to €700K annual revenue.

Now Product Lead at Humanz and fractional Head of Product at BankStore, an AI-native fintech. His skills come from P&Ls where one sale is one unique item and there is no second unit to make the margin back on.
