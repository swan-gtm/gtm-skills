# Which hire means "about to buy"

The role you search for is the one whose existence proves the problem. It is rarely
the buyer. Read the row, then contact the third column.

| They are hiring | It usually means | So the buyer is |
|---|---|---|
| first SDR / BDR | they are starting outbound and have no tooling | the founder or the Head of Sales |
| 2nd to 5th SDR | outbound works, they scale it, they will consolidate tools | Head of Sales, RevOps |
| first Head of Sales | the founder is handing off sales | the founder, before the hire lands |
| RevOps / Sales Ops | stack consolidation, data, attribution | the RevOps hire's boss, the VP Sales |
| Growth marketer / Demand gen | pipeline pressure from the board | CMO, founder |
| Content marketer | they will need distribution for what they write | Head of Marketing |
| Customer Success Manager | churn or expansion is the quarter's problem | VP CS, COO |
| Security / compliance engineer | a deal is blocked on a questionnaire | CTO, CISO |
| Data engineer, first | they cannot answer a question the board asked | CTO, Head of Data |
| Partnerships / alliances lead | direct sales has plateaued | CRO, founder |
| Product marketing manager, first | positioning is the blocker, not pipeline | CMO, CEO |

## Writing the row for a product

Ask: "what role does a company post the week before they would buy this?" Then:

- **What does the role's existence prove?** Not "they need sales tools" but "they have
  decided to do outbound and have nobody doing it yet".
- **Who signed the budget for that role?** That person is the buyer. The role itself
  reports to them.
- **When is the window?** Between the posting and the hire's first month. Once the hire
  lands, the hire chooses the tools and the buyer stops caring.

## First hire versus fifth hire

The same title means opposite things at different counts. Count the existing people
in the function on the company page before choosing the opener:

| Count already in role | What the new posting means | Opener angle |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | starting the motion, no tooling, founder still involved | what the first 90 days look like |
| 1 to 4 | it works, they scale, they will consolidate | what breaks between two and five |
| 5 and up | replacing churn or a reorg | usually not a signal; skip unless the JD names a new mandate |

## Signals that look like hiring signals and are not

- **Agency postings for a client**: the buyer is unknown and the agency will not tell
  you. Skip.
- **Evergreen postings** ("always hiring great SDRs"): no budget line behind them.
  Skip.
- **Internships**: no budget, no buyer. Skip.
- **A posting at a 5,000-person company for one more SDR**: the tool decision was made
  years ago. Skip unless the JD says "new team" or "greenfield".
