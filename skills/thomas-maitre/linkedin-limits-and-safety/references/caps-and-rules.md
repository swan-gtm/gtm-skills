# The caps, and the rules that cost more than the caps

Every number is per LinkedIn account per day, whatever sends the action.

## The caps

| Action | Per account, per day | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| Connection requests | 20 | LinkedIn's public guidance is about 100 a week; 20 a day on weekdays lands at 100 with no spikes |
| Direct messages | 40 | replies count; a conversation is many messages |
| Profile visits | 40 | the warm-up step; visits are the least sensitive action |
| Reactions | 30 | second warm-up step |
| Comments | 15 | comments are public and attributable; keep them rare and good |

Sum: 145 actions a day. Treat it as the ceiling for one account across every tool
that touches it, and every person who uses it. Two tools on one account do not each
get 145.

The reason to lower a cap is a new or thin account, never a crowded one:

| Account | Cap adjustment | For how long |
|---|---|---|
| under 3 months old | half of every cap | the first month of outreach |
| under 200 connections | half of every cap | until 200 connections |
| Premium or paid search seat | same caps; the note limit rises to 300 | permanent |
| coming back from a restriction | a quarter of every cap | two weeks, then half for two more |

## The rules that cost more than the caps when broken

- **Invitation notes are 200 characters on a free account, 300 on Premium.** LinkedIn
  does not negotiate: a 242-character note from a free account is rejected and the
  invite is never sent, silently. Write for 200 unless the account is known Premium.
- **Weekdays only.** A connection request on a Sunday morning is what gets you
  reported. Working hours matter less (a message read at 08:00 bothers nobody), but
  keeping to 08:00 to 19:00 in the account's own timezone costs nothing.
- **Warm up before inviting.** Visit the profile on day 0, react or comment on a post
  two days later, then invite. Skip posts older than 10 days; engaging with a
  two-month-old post looks like what it is.
- **Withdraw stale pending invites.** Over 3 weeks pending, withdraw. A pile of ignored
  requests is the strongest "this is automation" signal LinkedIn has, and it counts
  against the weekly limit.
- **Acceptance rate under 30 percent means stop and fix targeting**, not send more. The
  rate is invites accepted over invites sent, measured over 7 days.
- **Never search-and-invite from a fresh account.** Under 3 months old or under 200
  connections: half the caps for the first month, and no more than 10 invites a day.
- **One person's account, one person's voice.** Comments and messages written by
  someone else in a different register are noticed by the people who know the account
  holder, and those are the ones who report.
