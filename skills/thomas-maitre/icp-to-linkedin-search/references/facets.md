# The seven facets

Write the ICP as seven plain-word lists. Only name the facets the input gave or that
follow from the product; leave the rest empty. An empty facet is "any", not "unknown".

| Facet | What goes in | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `titles` | every wording of the role, current job only | CFO, Chief Financial Officer, VP Finance, Head of Finance, Finance Director |
| `seniority` | owner, cxo, vp, director, manager, senior, entry | cxo, vp, director |
| `languages` | the language the buyer posts in | French, English |
| `industries` | industry names as LinkedIn labels them; plain words are fine | Financial Services, Fintech, Banking |
| `sizes` | headcount bands | 11-50, 51-200, 201-500 |
| `locations` | countries, regions, metro areas | Switzerland, Geneva |
| `exclude` | never contact, absolute at every level | fractional, freelance, interim, intern, student, recruiter, agency, consultant |

## Headcount bands

`1-10`, `11-50`, `51-200`, `201-500`, `501-1000`, `1001-5000`, `5001-10000`, `10001+`.

"SMB" is 11 to 200. "Mid-market" is 201 to 1000. "Enterprise" is 1001 and up. When the
input says "startups", ask whether that means headcount (1 to 50) or funding stage; the
search can only filter on headcount.

## Reading a website URL

The buyer is on the site, in this order:

1. **Pricing page**: the size band. Per-seat pricing under 50 a month is SMB; "contact
   sales" with no numbers is mid-market and up; both on the same page means two ICPs,
   pick one.
2. **Customer logos**: the industry. Read the first six; the ones above the fold are the
   ones the company is proud of, which is the ICP it wants more of.
3. **Docs or integrations page**: the buyer's function. Integrations with a CRM mean a
   sales buyer; with an ad platform, marketing; with a data warehouse, RevOps or data.
4. **Careers page**, if the others are ambiguous: the roles the company hires for mirror
   the roles it sells to.

Write down which page each facet came from. When the card is questioned later, the
answer is a URL, not a memory.

## Seniority is a filter, not a title

`seniority` narrows the title family without adding words to it. "Head of Finance" at
`cxo, vp, director` excludes the "Head of Finance" who is a manager at a 5,000-person
company. Use it instead of adding "senior" to the string.
