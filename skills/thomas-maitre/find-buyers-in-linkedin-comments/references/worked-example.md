# Worked example: one intent, one day, one post

**Intent**: "people struggling with manual LinkedIn prospecting", French and English.

**Anchors**: the short and long names of the main paid LinkedIn search product, two
automation tools the buyer would name, "prospects froids", "liste de prospects",
"prospect list", "cold outreach".

**Buyer description**: anyone doing their own B2B prospecting. Exclude vendors, agencies,
consultants, recruiters, sales reps at prospecting tools.

## Search

Twenty 2 to 3 word queries, both languages, sorted by date, last 24 hours: 638 posts.
The sixteen pain-phrase queries written first had found 18 posts and missed the target.

## Engagement floor

`reactions + 2 x comments` above 20: 45 posts. Eleven above 100 reactions.

## The post that ranked first

"Help, I pay 100 a month for [the paid LinkedIn search product] and still build my lists
by hand." French, written by a founder, 96 reactions, 80 comments. Found by the two-word
short-form query for the product; the full product name did not find it.

Bucket: rant from someone who has the problem (first person, present tense, a number, a
plea). Score against the intent: 92. Kept.

## The people

150 unique engagers.

- Hard gates first: vendors, agencies, consultants and reps at prospecting tools
  removed. That is where most of the 150 went.
- Commenters scored against the intent. Five above 70. Examples of what scored:
  - "Same, and I still export to a spreadsheet every Monday" (intent 88)
  - "Great post" (intent 5, dropped)
  - "We solved this with our tool, DM me" (vendor, gated out before scoring)
- Reactors kept on fit only: role and seniority matching the buyer description.
- Author: read the profile. A founder doing their own sales and venting about it. Kept,
  and the best lead on the thread.

**Yield**: 22 fitting people, 5 with explicit intent, plus the author. Against the strict
ICP used for people search (Head of Sales, 11 to 200 employees, SaaS, one country), the
same post yields 1.

## The approach, for the five with intent

Day 0: visit the profile, react to the post, reply to their comment with one specific
sentence about what they wrote ("exporting every Monday is the part that never gets
automated; how big is the list by now?"). Day 2: invite, with a note that names the thread
in under 200 characters. No pitch in either. The conversation opens itself; the product
comes after they answer.

## What went wrong the first time

The first pass used the strict people-search ICP on engagers and reported "1 lead from a
post with 150 engagers", which reads as "this play does not work". The play was fine;
the fit rule was wrong for the source. Engagers are found by intent, so they are read
with a loose fit.
