# Gap plays

Each play closes a specific gap. Pick it when its trigger matches a weak answer and its roles include the leader's function. "Below 3" means any answer except the best option.

Format: **name** · roles · trigger · the workflow · the 30-day result.

## Pipeline creation

1. **Visitor-to-meeting on de-anonymized traffic** · marketing, RevOps, sales · Q11 or Q6 at 1 or lower · identify visiting companies, score them against the ICP, route to the owning rep with a researched brief and a drafted first touch · every ICP visit becomes a rep-ready account within an hour.
2. **Same-day event and webinar follow-up** · marketing · Q13 below 3 · attendee list in, enriched, one verified company fact per person, role-routed follow-up for the rep to approve · follow-up out the same day, replies tracked to pipeline.
3. **Buying-group signal graph** · marketing, RevOps · M1, M6 or Q6 below 3 · person-level activity resolved into account, role, buying group, stage and next action, without raw intent noise · the team sees which accounts are actually in market.
4. **Signal-ranked account queue** · sales · S2 or Q1 at 1 or lower · signals back-tested against recent wins drive a daily ranked queue with the reason to act now · reps start each day on the accounts most likely to move.
5. **Pipeline ownership model** · sales, founder · S1 below 3 · named owners and targets by segment and channel, reviewed weekly against contribution · no more "everyone prospect" weeks.
6. **Speed-to-lead on every marketing lead** · marketing, sales · M7 below 3 · every handed-over lead researched, routed and followed up within the hour with a drafted first touch, and tracked · no lead waits more than an hour.

## Making best practice runnable

7. **Top-rep playbook as a runnable skill** · sales · Q9, S5 or S6 below 3 · capture how the best rep researches, qualifies and writes, as a skill every rep runs on every account · new reps produce top-rep research on day one.
8. **New-rep ramp pack** · sales, founder · Q8 or S6 below 3 · account history, call notes, playbooks and objections assembled per territory on day one · ramp measured in weeks.
9. **Self-serve workflows with guardrails** · sales, RevOps, marketing · Q10, Q3 or Q25 below 3 · operators describe a play in plain language and get a working workflow with approval gates · three plays live without a RevOps ticket.
10. **New-motion launcher** · RevOps, founder · Q5 at 1 or lower · segment, signal, sequence and routing defined once and launched as a trigger, not a project · a new motion in days.
11. **Brand-safe AI content** · marketing · M5 below 3 · a versioned positioning and claims library every content workflow is tested against before publishing · AI content that sounds like one company.

## The shared layer

12. **One shared account brain** · all · Q7 or Q4 below 3 · one account record with ICP fit, history, signals and promises that marketing, sales, CS and RevOps all read and write · every handoff carries full context.
13. **Move the logic to one place** · RevOps, founder · logic test failed (logic split across systems) · pick the motion from the logic test, write its qualify, score, route and assign rules down in one place, and make the systems execute that one definition · a scoring change ships in a day, by the motion's owner.
14. **Evidence-backed CRM writeback** · RevOps, sales · Q15, S4, R2 or R3 below 3 · call and signal outputs enter the CRM through staging with source, timestamp, confidence and deduplication · a CRM you would put in a board deck without a cleanup week.
15. **Tool consolidation map** · RevOps · Q14 at 1 or lower · map each daily tool to the step it serves, then collapse enrichment, sequencing and routing into one orchestration layer · a plan with named tools to retire.

## Customers

16. **Expansion signals to the CSM with full history** · customer success, sales · Q21 or Q22 below 3 · usage, hiring, new-exec and champion-move signals routed to the CSM with the deal, promises and tickets in one brief · expansion conversations that start informed.
17. **Renewal-risk watch 90 days out** · customer success · Q23 or C3 below 3 · usage drop, champion departure and support sentiment watched per account, play launched at 90 days · every renewal has a risk read before the call.
18. **Sales-to-CS handoff brief** · customer success, sales · Q24 below 3 · the deal record, call notes and promises turned into a handoff brief the moment a deal closes · no CSM asks "what did we promise?"
19. **Customer value-story engine** · customer success · C2 below 3 · usage and outcome metrics produce gated value summaries for lifecycle touches and QBRs · every customer sees their own ROI before renewal.
20. **ARR event model** · customer success, RevOps · C1 below 3 · renewals, expansions, contractions and churn modeled once and reconciled across CRM and billing · one retention number everyone trusts.

## Running AI in production

21. **AI workflow production gate** · all · J1 at AI as an action engine or above, or N3, N5, R4 or E2 below 3 · inventory live AI workflows and give each an owner, an eval set, an approval threshold, rollback and a cost ceiling · every AI workflow in production has an owner and a kill switch.
22. **Change-control monitor** · RevOps, founder · N2 or R4 below 3 · regression tests run automatically when models, prompts, schemas or vendor APIs change · breakage found by a test, not by a customer.
23. **AI unit economics ledger** · founder, RevOps, customer success · N4, E4 or E1 below 3 · cost per run tied to accepted outputs, meetings, opportunities, saves and expansions · a cost-per-outcome number for the next budget review.
24. **AI-to-revenue proof** · founder, marketing · N6 or Q12 at 1 or lower · one workflow measured against a baseline or holdout through pipeline and revenue · a defensible ROI line instead of activity counts.
25. **Data access policy for AI** · all · N1 below 3 · data classes, least-privilege access and audit logs defined for each AI workflow · security signs off once, not per tool.
26. **Headcount versus agents plan** · founder · E4 below 3 · the next hire's week mapped into tasks, the agent-covered tasks removed, the human role redefined · a hiring plan the board understands.

## If every answer is strong

Say in one line that the basics are in place, then pick from plays 21 to 25. At 04 Shared AI layer the work is keeping production AI owned, tested and measured.
