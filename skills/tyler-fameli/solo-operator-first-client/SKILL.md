Applies when one person sells bounded technical work (automation, integrations, AI workflows, infrastructure projects), has proof they built themselves but no client results, and is acquiring through warm referrals and a freelance marketplace. Produces a scored queue, human-approved drafts and a tally that separates channel problems from demand problems.

## The play

1. **Load history before sourcing anything.** Read prior dispositions, contact history, opt-outs and sent records. Never revive a disqualified or archived lead silently; never re-pitch someone already contacted without reading what was sent. Deduplicate before any draft.
2. **Source from two channels only.** Warm network: people who already know the operator's work. Marketplace: fresh postings that describe a concrete broken flow or manual burden. Cold email blasts, calls and paid boosts are out of this play.
3. **Run the first screen.** Ask: what reproducible gap is left after the buyer's existing tools and owner have done their job? Check native features, existing connectors and plain process fixes first. If configuration solves it, that is the recommendation, and a no-build answer is a valid result.
4. **Apply hard stops before scoring.** Disqualify on evidenced ineligibility (location, credential), staffing disguised as a project (hourly seat, 30+ hrs/week, "own our systems"), unfunded solve-it-first or conditional-payment demands, unsafe access requests, and anything that needs invented experience to win. No score overrides a hard stop.
5. **Score fit and intent separately.** Fit asks "can this operator deliver this bounded thing"; intent asks "does this buyer want it, with money and a decision-maker." Pursue only at fit >= 70, intent >= 60, composite >= 70. Any unknown input forces HOLD; it is never scored as zero or as positive. Read `references/qualification-rubric.md` for weights, 0/50/100 anchors and hard stops.
6. **Scope the entry offer to the buyer's size.** Same problem family, different offer: one flow between two systems for a tiny business, monitored failures added for a small company, one department under its own security and change control for an enterprise. Uncertain scope gets a paid diagnostic before any implementation quote. Publish no price menu; quote each bounded scope individually.
7. **Draft per recipient, then queue for human approval.** One problem in their words, one detail proving the post or situation was read, the bounded approach, relevant self-built proof, one next step. State the preference for an ongoing partnership once, plainly, in one sentence. Read `references/channel-playbooks.md` for the warm ask, the bid structure and follow-up limits.
8. **A human approves and sends each message.** Batch the review (one packet per channel per day), but approval and sending stay per recipient. A changed message needs fresh approval. Record "sent" only after confirming it went out.
9. **Tally with denominators and review every 10 sent attempts.** Count only observed events per channel: sent, replies, qualified conversations, paid diagnostics. Read `references/experiment-and-economics.md` for volume caps, spend limits, the contribution floor and stop/continue rules.

## What good looks like

- The best operators notice insourcing first: a company hiring an integration owner is building the capability in-house, so the posting is a HOLD, not a lead. Growth, funding and platform adoption are hypotheses, not buying intent.
- Solve-it-first wording, "minimum payment if it works" and demands for two production examples of the exact project are the tells of an unfunded or trust-gated buyer. Pass, or offer a funded diagnostic milestone.
- The mediocre version is volume without a gate: generic "AI transformation" proposals, intent inflated from relevance, custom builds pitched before native options, drafts counted as traction, and "no demand" concluded from a handful of silent sends.
- Good output is reproducible: another person can rebuild every score from the stated evidence, see each unknown, and explain why this recipient, this bounded offer and this next step. Read `references/worked-examples.md` for scored cases, including the time relevance was mistaken for qualification.
- Claims stay inside what the operator has actually built. Willingness to learn is stated as willingness to learn.

## Rules

- MUST read contact history and dispositions before drafting; MUST preserve opt-outs, sent records and human edits.
- MUST keep every external message behind per-recipient human approval and a human send.
- MUST label unknowns as unknown; MUST count outcomes with denominators.
- NEVER auto-send, buy reviews, use paid boosts, or bypass a suppression.
- NEVER invent clients, results, savings, certifications or portfolio pieces to meet a posting's requirement.
- NEVER accept unfunded work, open-ended correctness liability, or an hourly staffing seat dressed as a project to hit volume.
